Beta v0.1.7 fixes the Super jump and Double jump cheats.

In Beta v0.1.5 and v0.1.6, Super jump raised the camera instead of the jump,
and the raised view could stay after the option was turned off. Double jump
had the same fault. **Both now act on the real jump:**

- **Super jump** launches about 2.5x higher than a normal jump.
- **Double jump** gives one extra full jump in the air (a super jump if both
  are on), and resets when you land.
- A camera left raised by the earlier versions, including in saved games,
  returns to normal height automatically.

All 15 title checks pass, and the fix was tested in game in Suburbs with both
cheats on and off.

Extract over the previous release while keeping your game folder, saves and
settings. Keep `d3dcompiler_ms.dll` beside `serious_sam_recomp.exe`, then start
the executable normally. You need your own original Xbox **USA** game files;
no game data or personal settings are included.

Everything from Beta v0.1.6 is included. Previously reported boot,
shader/compiler and other stability issues remain under investigation.
Full-game validation is pending. For crashes or freezes, include
`serious_sam_log.txt`; F11 captures help with rendering reports.
