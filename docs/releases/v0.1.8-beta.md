Beta v0.1.8 adds an optional controller stick deadzone.

The original game ignores roughly the first 40% of each stick direction, and
because it does this per axis, small diagonal movements snap to straight lines.
That is how the Xbox release behaves, so it remains the default.

**Options > PC Controls > Stick deadzone** now offers:

- **Original** (default): the game's own deadzone, unchanged.
- **5%, 10%, 15% or 20%**: a round deadzone. Small stick movements respond
  smoothly from just past the deadzone, diagonals keep both directions, and
  full diagonal movement keeps its original speed.

If your controller drifts at a low setting, choose a higher one. The choice is
saved with your other PC controls, and Restore defaults returns to Original.

All 16 title checks pass, including a model of the game's own two deadzone
stages, and the 10% setting was tested in game with a controller.

Extract over the previous release while keeping your game folder, saves and
settings. Keep `d3dcompiler_ms.dll` beside `serious_sam_recomp.exe`, then start
the executable normally. You need your own original Xbox **USA** game files;
no game data or personal settings are included.

Everything from Beta v0.1.7 is included. Previously reported boot,
shader/compiler and other stability issues remain under investigation.
Full-game validation is pending. For crashes or freezes, include
`serious_sam_log.txt`; F11 captures help with rendering reports.
