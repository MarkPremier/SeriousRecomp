Beta v0.1.6 fixes walker shadow visibility on normal executable launches.

In Beta v0.1.5, full-strength walker shadows required the separate test
launcher. Starting `serious_sam_recomp.exe` directly still used the faint
original strength. **Full-strength biomechanical walker shadows are now on
by default.** No special launcher or environment setting is required.

Other enemies keep their native shadows. The original soft-edged blob
texture, receiver projection, fog and depth testing remain in use. Set
`RECOMP_WALKER_SHADOW_MIN=0` to restore the original lighting-dependent
strength for comparison. This remains a visibility adjustment; original
Xbox visual parity has not been established.

All 15 title checks pass. A direct launch with the shadow environment variable
absent was verified in a copied Suburbs save: the startup log reports the
enabled default, F11 records full RGB strength, and the game window shows
the walker's ground shadow. Original saves and settings were unchanged by
the private test.

Extract over the previous release while keeping your game folder, saves and
settings. Keep `d3dcompiler_ms.dll` beside `serious_sam_recomp.exe`, then start
the executable normally. You need your own original Xbox **USA** game files;
no game data or personal settings are included.

Cheats and menu fixes from Beta v0.1.5 are included. Previously reported boot,
shader/compiler and other stability issues remain under investigation, as
does the Suburbs floor texture/lighting seam. Full-game validation is pending.
For crashes or freezes, include `serious_sam_log.txt`; F11 captures help with
rendering reports.
